# A 15-minute winter-surface decision card

Use this card for a private walkway, steps or driveway. It is not a road-maintenance plan and does not guarantee safe footing.

## 1. Observe before choosing

- Surface: `walkway / steps / driveway / other: __________`
- Hazard: `loose snow / slush / thin bonded ice / refreeze / unknown`
- Pavement temperature, if you can measure it: `__________`
- Air temperature and trend: `__________`
- Product label's stated range and warnings: `__________`

If you cannot determine the pavement temperature, record that uncertainty instead of treating the air reading as a substitute.

## 2. Remove the bulk

Can loose snow or slush be cleared safely now? `yes / no`

Action taken: `shovel / sweep / scrape / wait / ask for help`

Stop if the work, weather or surface makes clearing unsafe for you. Do not chip in a way that damages the surface or sends fragments toward people.

## 3. Name the remaining job

Choose one primary job:

- `Melt or loosen a thin bonded layer` — follow the deicer label and local guidance.
- `Add temporary traction` — use an appropriate abrasive, knowing it will not melt ice.
- `Prevent access and wait` — mark or block the hazard until conditions or help change.

Why this job fits: `__________________________________________________`

## 4. Use the smallest justified treatment

Material and label instruction: `________________________________________`

Amount or coverage chosen from the label: `______________________________`

Do not add more merely because melting is slow. Ordinary sodium chloride works much more slowly as pavement gets colder and is generally impractical below about 15°F.

## 5. Recheck and clean up

- Recheck time: `__________`
- Remaining slippery spots: `___________________________________________`
- Refreeze risk or new precipitation: `__________________________________`
- Leftover material swept up when practical: `yes / no / not yet`

**Success:** Bulk snow is removed where safe, the remaining hazard has a deliberate treatment, and the surface will be rechecked rather than assumed safe.
