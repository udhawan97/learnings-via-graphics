# Pay-stub review card

[Visual brief](brief.md) · [Full guide](index.html) · [Evidence and limitations](sources.md)

Use this private worksheet with one pay statement. Do not paste account numbers, tax IDs, addresses or the full statement into a public document.

## 1. Identify the statement

- Employee name matches: `yes / ask payroll`
- Employer matches: `yes / ask payroll`
- Pay period: `__________ to __________`
- Payment date: `__________`
- Pay frequency: `weekly / every two weeks / twice monthly / monthly / other: ______`

## 4. Reconcile the paycheck

`current gross pay $______ + current additions $______ − current deductions and withholdings $______ = current net pay $______`

- The equation matches the statement: `yes / no`
- Deposit or check amount matches current net pay: `yes / no / split deposit`

## 5. Read year-to-date separately

Choose one line and compare its current and year-to-date amounts. A year-to-date figure accumulates across pay periods; it is not another subtraction from this paycheck.

- Line reviewed: `__________`
- Current amount: `$__________`
- Year-to-date amount: `$__________`
- Difference I need explained: `none / ____________________`

## Finish safely

Success means the current arithmetic reconciles and every current addition or subtraction has a label you understand. Ask payroll about an unfamiliar line or mismatch. For withholding questions, use the official [IRS tax-withholding guidance](https://www.irs.gov/payments/tax-withholding) or a qualified adviser. This worksheet does not determine whether a deduction is lawful or calculate personalized tax.
