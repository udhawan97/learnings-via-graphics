# Job-evidence map

[Visual brief](brief.md) · [Full guide](index.html) · [Evidence](sources.md)

Use one real job posting and your own records. Estimated time: 20 minutes. Do not paste private employer, client, customer, health, or financial information into this worksheet.

## 1. Choose three priority lines

Copy the employer's wording, then classify it. A criterion is **unclear** when the posting does not say whether it is mandatory.

| Posting requirement | Required / preferred / unclear | Why it appears important |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. `________________________________` | `____________` | `________________________` |
| 2. `________________________________` | `____________` | `________________________` |
| 3. `________________________________` | `____________` | `________________________` |

## 2. Build the evidence chain

| Requirement | Truthful claim | Specific example | Inspectable signal | Gap or next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `____________` | `____________` | `____________` | `____________` |
| 2 | `____________` | `____________` | `____________` | `____________` |
| 3 | `____________` | `____________` | `____________` | `____________` |

An inspectable signal might be a result in your records, a redacted work sample, a date and scope you can explain, or a reference who has agreed to be contacted. “Hard worker” is not a signal by itself.

## 3. Test each draft line

For every claim, check:

- [ ] I did this work or made this contribution.
- [ ] I can explain the situation, my action, and the result.
- [ ] Any number comes from a record or a clearly labeled estimate.
- [ ] The supporting material is mine to share and contains no confidential data.
- [ ] A missing requirement remains visible.

## 4. Choose a next action

For each gap, choose one: `learn` · `ask for clarification` · `apply and disclose honestly` · `skip this opening`.

**Observable success:** A reader can point from each targeted resume claim to a real example and safe supporting signal, and can identify every unresolved required criterion without guessing.
